# Lite report schema

1. Decision and strongest blocker
2. Product definition and benchmark ASINs
3. Evidence-state matrix
4. Direct-market scope and exclusions
5. Demand and competition structure
6. New-entrant evidence
7. Winner and failure analysis
8. Basic economics
9. IP, compliance and safety screen
10. Evidence required for deeper validation

Never include a purchase quantity, success probability, or procurement approval in a Lite report.
