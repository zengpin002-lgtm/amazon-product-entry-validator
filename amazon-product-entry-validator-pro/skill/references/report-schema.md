# Purchase-grade report schema

1. Decision level and strongest veto
2. Visual overview with benchmark ASINs and sourced images
3. Evidence supports / does not support
4. Store entry strategy and trial constraints
5. Evidence completeness matrix
6. Direct-market definition and exclusions
7. Demand, trend, seasonality and region
8. Product, brand, seller, Top 3/10 and new-product structure
9. New-brand versus old-brand success
10. Winner assets and store reproducibility
11. Failure, VOC, return and QC risks
12. Price, promotion, traffic, parent and variant analysis
13. Packaging, FBA and landed cost
14. Base and stress profit scenarios
15. IP, compliance, safety and supplier gates
16. Hard gates, then weighted score
17. 50-unit trial, 100-unit cap, cash exposure and exit
18. Upgrade, downgrade and stop conditions
19. Sources, dates, definitions, conflicts and blocked fields
