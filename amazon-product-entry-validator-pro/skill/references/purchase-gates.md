# Purchase-grade gates

## Market

- Verify direct substitutes, exclusions, time window, parent/child treatment and sample size.
- Reconcile broad-category and direct-market concentration.
- Treat direct-market concentration near or above 50% as an entry blocker unless multiple low-review new brands gained durable, reproducible share.
- Verify product, brand, seller, Top 3/10, new-product count and new-product sales share.

## Reproducibility

- Classify traffic, parent/variant, review, price, promotion, images, video/3D, keywords, delivery and page assets.
- Separate reproducible advantages from historical or paid assets the target store cannot copy.

## Economics

- Verify final package dimensions and weight.
- Use third-party FBA estimates for screening and Amazon official fees for purchase decisions.
- Include referral, FBA, inbound placement, storage, product, packaging, freight, duty, advertising, promotion, returns, damage and clearance.
- Require positive conservative contribution and a defined inventory exit.

## Risk

- Reject red IP, compliance or safety risk.
- Hold unsupported central performance claims.
- Hold when supplier, sample, packaging, QC or freight evidence is insufficient for the requested action.

## Decision ceiling

- Third-party FBA only: maximum `CONDITIONAL SAMPLE`.
- No supplier quote or sample: maximum `CONDITIONAL SAMPLE`.
- Unresolved concentration scope: `DATA INCOMPLETE`.
- High concentration without durable new-brand entry: `ENTRY_BLOCKED`.
- Negative conservative profit: `LOSS`.
- No 50-unit exit path: `CASH_RISK`.
