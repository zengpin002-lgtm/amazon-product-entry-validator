# Amazon Product Entry Validator Pro

Private commercial Codex Skill for purchase-grade Amazon product validation.

## Commercial capabilities

- direct-market scope and concentration conflict resolution;
- SellerSprite batch-data contract and listing-level cross-check;
- new-brand versus old-brand entry validation;
- winner traffic and Listing reproducibility;
- Amazon-official FBA fee reconciliation;
- landed cost and multi-scenario profit calculation;
- 50-unit inventory exit and 100-unit upper-limit gates;
- IP, compliance, supplier, sample, packaging, and QC gates;
- purchase-grade reports with auditable evidence states.

## Decision levels

`PASS`, `CONDITIONAL SAMPLE`, `HOLD FOR PURCHASE`, `DATA INCOMPLETE`, `ENTRY_BLOCKED`, `CASH_RISK`, `LOSS`, or `REJECT`.

## Distribution

This repository is private commercial source. Grant customer access only under a paid license. Do not place store exports, credentials, supplier quotes, candidate reports, or customer learning records in this repository.

## Validation

Validate the Skill with Codex `quick_validate.py`, then test the gate, landed-cost, and profit scripts before each release.
